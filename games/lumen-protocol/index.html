HOW IT WORKS

  • READ & CHOOSE — click or [SPACE] advances. Numbered choices: click or press [1..9]. Greyed choices show the trait/skill they want; they unlock as you change.
  • YOU ARE THE STATS — EMPATHY (open), LUCIDITY (cold-clear), DEFIANCE (edit the rules), RELIC (how much of everyone else lives in you). Choices move them permanently. Your PERSONA title and available options follow.
  • SKILLS ARE PERMANENT — ECHO SIGHT lets you catch contradictions; FROST READ, FLOOD TONGUE, STEEL MEMORY and friends gate whole branches. Some skills can be lost. Loss is also content.
  • CARGO — packages ride in the tray (bottom-right). Carried minds whisper in the ticker and change scenes. Deliver the ORIGINAL (whole to them, gone from you) or a COPY (they get 92%, you keep a ghost — and it sharpens you).
  • OFFERINGS — feeding a grief back to the city (you'll know when) calms weather and buys latitude.
  • ENDINGS — the final roof offers RETURN / INTEGRATE / BROADCAST / GLITCH. Which are open depends on who you became on the way up. Autosaves per scene; CONTINUE recovers mid-run.
