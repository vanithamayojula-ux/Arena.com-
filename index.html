    Vaelune only surfaces when the sun is eaten.
    You have until totality to remember it.

    A half-sunken city of lamps and markets, glowing from underneath, that appears only during a solar eclipse — and only while somebody remembers it truly.

    You are the Archivist, the one who stayed awake. Six of Vaelune's people are still down there, each holding one fragment of its final day.

    How to play

    Getting around

    W A S D walk, wade and swim. Water slows you down; deep water makes you swim.

    space steps up onto kerbs and stairs, lifts you while swimming, and hauls you out of the water onto a ledge. C dives.

    shift hurries — only where the water is shallow. mouse looks; click to capture the pointer, or just drag. Arrow keys steer too.

    Remembering

    Each fragment of the city's last day is held by one of its people. Walk to the light of a fragment, stand close, and hold E until the ring fills.

    What you restore comes back: stalls and voices in the market, a turning lamp at the observatory, lanterns rising out of the amphitheatre.

    Dark memories cost something — they raise shadows that drain your lucidity. Hold your lantern on one and it dissolves.

    Staying alive, and the tide

    F raises the lantern. It burns oil, and nothing dark will come near the light. Restoring a memory refills the oil.

    Lucidity is your grip on the memory. Lose all of it and you wake on the plaza, no further along — there is no death here, only falling behind.

    Every fragment you restore raises the tide. Routes you walked dry at the start will be chest-deep by the end. The city goes under at totality.
